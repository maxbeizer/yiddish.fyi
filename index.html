🚀 Usage

  • CORS enabled - works from browsers
  • No authentication - just hit the endpoint
  • No rate limits - (within reason, don't be a schmuck)
  • Free forever - runs on Cloudflare Workers free tier

💡 Use Cases

  • Word-of-the-day widgets
  • Home Assistant dashboards
  • Tidbyt displays
  • Learning apps
  • Just for fun
